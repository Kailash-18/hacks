THE PHYSICAL LAB PHOTO-LED REVERSE ENGINEERING

Look closer.
Understand how it fits.

Turn object photos into an interactive study. Trace each visible part to its source, distinguish evidence from inference, and explore the relationships between components.

01 / PHOTO EVIDENCE

Begin with clear views.

Use up to four angles of one object. Keep visible components tied to their source image and separate from parts inferred behind the surface.

02 / DEPTH VIEW

Move beyond a flat image.

Arrange photo cutouts as depth layers in the browser. Rotate the scene, isolate a part, and examine the object's visible form from new angles.

03 / PART CONNECTIONS

See how components relate.

Open part details, follow linked components, and explore explanations based on the selected study. Estimated depth and hidden parts remain clearly identified.
