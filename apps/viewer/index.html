Upload 1–4 photos of any object. The AI finds its parts, cuts them out, estimates depth and pulls them apart so you can inspect each one. More angles = more real parts.

  • One object, filling most of the frame
  • Plain background, good light
  • Objects with visible parts work best: a bike, a keyboard, an open PC, a drone
  • Add a bottom or opened-up shot to reveal parts hidden from the top
