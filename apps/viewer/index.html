What one photo gives you

  1. Photo in

    One to four shots of the same object. Each extra angle finds parts the others missed.

  2. Parts named

    Every part you can see is cut out of your photo, labelled and checked against the real product.

  3. Insides filled in

    Boards, batteries and sensors you can't see are added from what this product really contains.

  4. Pull it apart

    Explode the 3D model, X-ray the wiring, or remove a part to see what stops working.
